```
### Page 9: Path Graph

**Matching screenshot:** Your uploaded image showing the full interactive path graph with Explore/Target toggle, "You Now" at top, branching to multiple cards at bottom

**Components used:**
- React Flow canvas as the main area
- **Mode toggle** in top-right: "Explore" / "Target" tabs — selected tab has `#0e6602` text and underline

  - Border: `#0e6602` for ready cards, blue for medium, gray for far
- **Edges (arrows):** Curved Bezier paths in light gray. On hover: glow green `#0e6602`. On click: expand detail panel.
- **Zoom controls** bottom-right: Three circular buttons (zoom in, fit, zoom out) with `#483C32` icons on white backgrounds

**Path graph layout for Explore mode (matching your screenshot):**
```
              [You Now]

   [Amex Blue Cash P...]  [Capital One Vent...]
